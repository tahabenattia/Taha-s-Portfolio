Master's Thesis: Emotional Misalignment in Contemporary Music

Research Question: How often does a song's audio "feel" the same emotion as its lyrics — and when it doesn't, what patterns explain the mismatch?

My Approach: Analyzed 1,080 songs across 12 genres using K-means clustering on Spotify audio features (valence, energy, danceability, etc.) alongside GPT-4-based emotion classification of the lyrics, then measured how often the two signals agreed.

Key Findings:
• Misalignment is the norm, not the exception — only 46% of songs showed audio-lyrical alignment, while 54% showed systematic contrast
• Statistically significant relationship between the two modalities (χ² = 99.45, p < 0.001), but only slight practical agreement (κ = 0.164)
• Distinct genre strategies: hip-hop pairs angry lyrics with happy-sounding audio (55% mismatch), jazz pairs happy lyrics with sad-sounding audio (92% mismatch), metal pairs sad lyrics with angry-sounding audio (66% mismatch)

Context: Master's thesis in Data Science and Advanced Analytics, NOVA IMS (June 2025).

Skills Demonstrated: Python, NLP, LLM-based classification, K-means Clustering, Statistical Testing, API Data Collection
